This prototype presents Tore as a digital money app. Final regulatory wording and partner details should be added before production.

Download the app, follow the identity checks, and you can be ready to go in just a few minutes.

The example core account has no monthly fee. Any paid plan or optional service would be shown clearly before you choose it.

How is my money protected?＋

The app uses encryption, secure authentication, and real-time fraud monitoring to protect account access.

Can I use Tore abroad?＋

Yes. You can pay while travelling and see exchange information before each transaction.
